GET STARTED

Ready in
a few taps.

This app download is for compatible Android phones and tablets.
An internet connection is required.

  1. 1

    Download the APK

    Tap “Download for Android” and wait for KAHE-Attendance-1.0.6.apk to finish downloading.

  2. 2

    Open and install

    Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow your browser to install this app, then tap Install. If the app is already installed, tap Update; you do not need to uninstall it first.

  3. 3

    Sign in to your account

    Use the account provided by your institution. If login approval is requested, contact your batch administrator.
